# Master Services Agreement — Bellmark Distribution Inc.

**Contract ID:** NX-2025-0114
**Client:** Bellmark Distribution Inc., 4400 Winfield Road, Columbus, Ohio 43215, USA
**Products licensed:** RouteAI (Enterprise tier), TrackPulse (add-on)
**Contract value:** $1,840,000 USD annually
**Total committed value:** $5,520,000 USD over the initial term
**Start date:** 1 April 2025
**Initial term:** 36 months, ending 31 March 2028

## Scope

Bellmark licenses RouteAI for 3,200 active vehicles across 14 distribution centres in the US Midwest, plus TrackPulse for an estimated 6.4 million tracked shipments per year. Pricing is $41 per vehicle per month under the 500+ volume tier, with TrackPulse billed at $0.09 per shipment under a negotiated enterprise rate.

## Renewal terms

The agreement auto-renews for successive 24-month terms unless either party gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term. On renewal, fees may increase by no more than 4% or the prior year's US CPI-U, whichever is lower. Bellmark holds a one-time right to reduce licensed vehicle count by up to 15% at the first renewal without penalty.

## Service levels and other terms

Platform uptime commitment is 99.9% monthly, with service credits of 5% of monthly fees per 0.1% shortfall, capped at 25%. Payment terms are net 45, billed quarterly in advance. Data processing is governed by the DPA executed 1 April 2025. Termination for convenience is not permitted during the initial term.
