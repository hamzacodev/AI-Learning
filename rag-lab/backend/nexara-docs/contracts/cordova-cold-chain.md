# Software Subscription Agreement — Cordova Cold Chain S.A.

**Contract ID:** NX-2024-0287
**Client:** Cordova Cold Chain S.A., Carrer de la Marina 118, 08018 Barcelona, Spain
**Products licensed:** RouteAI (Enterprise tier with cold-chain constraint module), TrackPulse
**Contract value:** €612,000 EUR annually
**Total committed value:** €1,224,000 EUR over the initial term
**Start date:** 1 December 2024
**Initial term:** 24 months, ending 30 November 2026

## Scope

Cordova licenses RouteAI for 940 refrigerated vehicles operating across Spain, Portugal, and southern France. The engagement includes the cold-chain constraint module, which enforces temperature windows per shipment leg and blocks route plans that would breach a consignment's permitted excursion budget. TrackPulse covers 1.9 million tracked shipments annually with temperature-excursion alerting enabled on all lanes.

Fees break down as €480,000 for RouteAI (€42.55 per vehicle per month) and €132,000 for TrackPulse, inclusive of a €45,000 one-time implementation fee amortized across year one.

## Renewal terms

The agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice before term end. Renewal uplift is capped at 3.5% annually. Cordova may add vehicles mid-term at the contracted per-vehicle rate; reductions take effect only at renewal.

## Service levels and other terms

Uptime commitment is 99.9% monthly. Payment terms are net 30, billed annually in advance. GDPR data processing terms are attached as Schedule 2, with all data resident in eu-west-1. Implementation was led by Tomás Ferreira and completed on 18 December 2024. Cordova's signatory was Alejandro Ruiz-Ibáñez, Director General.
