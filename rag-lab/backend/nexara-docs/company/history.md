# Nexara — Company History

## 2017

Nexara incorporated in Rotterdam on 14 March 2017. Ingrid Halvorsen and Hamza Shakoor raised a €1.2 million pre-seed round in September 2017 led by Vasten Ventures, with participation from angel investor Pieter Roosmalen.

## 2018

First commercial pilot signed with Kestrel Freight BV in April 2018 covering 120 vehicles. The RouteAI engine shipped its first production version, v0.9, in November 2018.

## 2019

Seed round of €6.5 million closed in June 2019, led by Northbend Capital. Headcount grew from 14 to 41. Nexara opened its Austin, Texas office in October 2019.

## 2020

Revenue grew 210% despite pandemic disruption as customers used RouteAI to re-plan around border closures. Crossed 50 paying customers in August 2020.

## 2021

Series A of €24 million in March 2021, led by Aureon Partners with Northbend Capital following on. TrackPulse launched in public beta on 8 September 2021.

## 2022

Crossed €10 million ARR in February 2022. Acquired Vessent Analytics, a five-person Ghent-based ETA-prediction startup, for €3.1 million in July 2022.

## 2023

Series B of €78 million closed in May 2023 at a €410 million post-money valuation, led by Halberd Growth Fund. Headcount reached 160.

## 2024

SOC 2 Type II certification achieved in January 2024. Opened Singapore sales office in June 2024. ARR reached €41.7 million.

## 2025

ARR reached €62.4 million. Announced first profitable quarter in Q4 2025.
