# Nexara — Company Overview

Nexara is an AI-powered logistics platform headquartered in Rotterdam, Netherlands, with a secondary engineering office in Austin, Texas. The company was founded in March 2017 by Ingrid Halvorsen, a former supply-chain operations lead at Maersk, together with co-founder Hamza Shakoor, who previously built forecasting systems at Zalando.

The founding story began at the Port of Rotterdam in late 2016. Halvorsen was auditing a container backlog and found that 38% of delayed shipments could have been rerouted profitably if dispatchers had access to live traffic, weather, and port-congestion data in a single view. She spent four months prototyping a routing engine on her own laptop, then recruited Bhatnagar in January 2017 after meeting him at a logistics conference in Hamburg.

Nexara's mission is stated internally as: "Move every shipment along the route it should have taken." The company builds decision systems for mid-market freight operators — carriers running between 50 and 5,000 vehicles — who cannot afford the custom optimization teams that large 3PLs maintain in-house.

As of Q2 2026, Nexara employs 214 people across 11 countries, with 96 in engineering. The platform processes approximately 4.1 million routing decisions per day and serves 340 active enterprise customers. Annual recurring revenue reached €62.4 million in 2025.

The company operates on a remote-first model with quarterly onsite gatherings. Its two commercial products are RouteAI and TrackPulse. Ingrid Halvorsen remains CEO; Hamza Shakoor serves as Chief Technology Officer.
