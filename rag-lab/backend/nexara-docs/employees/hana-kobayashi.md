# Hana Kobayashi — Staff Machine Learning Engineer

**Name:** Hana Kobayashi
**Role:** Staff Machine Learning Engineer
**Join date:** 3 February 2022
**Product team:** TrackPulse (ETA Prediction squad)
**Location:** Singapore
**Reports to:** Sofia Wenzel, Head of Product, TrackPulse

Hana Kobayashi is the technical lead for TrackPulse's predictive ETA engine. She joined Nexara on 3 February 2022 as a senior ML engineer and was promoted to staff in August 2024. She was the first Nexara employee based in Asia-Pacific, predating the Singapore sales office opening in June 2024 by more than two years.

Hana owns the ocean-leg arrival model, which blends AIS vessel positions, historical berth-wait distributions at 190 ports, and weather routing data. Her rework of the model's port-congestion features in Q1 2025 cut transatlantic ETA mean absolute error from 6.8 hours to 4.1 hours.

Before Nexara she worked for five years at Grab in Singapore on demand forecasting, and briefly at Rakuten in Tokyo. She holds an MEng in Computer Science from Kyoto University (2015).

She maintains Nexara's internal feature store, `pulse-features`, used by both TrackPulse and RouteAI, and runs the model-drift review each month. Her 2026 goal is per-shipment confidence intervals rather than point ETA estimates, scheduled for the TrackPulse 5.0 release in August 2026.
