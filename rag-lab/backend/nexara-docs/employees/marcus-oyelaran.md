# Marcus Oyelaran — Director of Engineering, RouteAI

**Name:** Marcus Oyelaran
**Role:** Director of Engineering, Optimization
**Join date:** 6 May 2019
**Product team:** RouteAI
**Location:** Rotterdam, Netherlands (hybrid)
**Reports to:** Hamza Shakoor, CTO

Marcus Oyelaran leads the 34-person RouteAI engineering organization, covering the solver core, the travel-time model, and the constraint library. He joined Nexara in May 2019 as a senior optimization engineer — employee number 23 — and was promoted to Director of Engineering in November 2022.

Before Nexara he spent six years at ORTEC in Zoetermeer building vehicle-routing software for retail distribution, and holds a PhD in Operations Research from Delft University of Technology (2013), where his thesis covered stochastic vehicle routing under uncertain travel times.

Marcus owns the adaptive large neighbourhood search rewrite shipped in RouteAI v3.0 (April 2023), which cut median solve latency for 200-stop problems from 1.9 seconds to 340 milliseconds. He also chairs Nexara's internal Solver Review Board, which signs off on any change affecting customer-visible route cost.

His current priority for 2026 is multi-depot consolidation planning, targeted for RouteAI v4.2 in Q3 2026. He mentors three engineers through the company's staff-engineer track and runs the monthly "Solver Deep Dive" internal talk series.
