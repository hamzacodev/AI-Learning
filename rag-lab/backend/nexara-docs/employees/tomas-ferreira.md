# Tomás Ferreira — Senior Solutions Architect

**Name:** Tomás Ferreira
**Role:** Senior Solutions Architect, Enterprise
**Join date:** 19 July 2021
**Product team:** RouteAI (customer-facing deployment team)
**Location:** Austin, Texas, USA
**Reports to:** Marcus Oyelaran, Director of Engineering, Optimization

Tomás Ferreira leads technical onboarding for Nexara's largest RouteAI accounts in North America. He joined on 19 July 2021 as the fourth hire in the Austin office and has since run 31 enterprise deployments, including the Bellmark Distribution rollout completed in March 2025 and the Cordova Cold Chain implementation in late 2024.

His work covers constraint modelling — translating a customer's real-world dispatch rules into RouteAI's 47 constraint types — plus data-feed integration, pilot design, and the go-live cutover. He wrote Nexara's standard 90-day deployment playbook, now used by the whole solutions team.

Before Nexara, Tomás spent seven years at C.H. Robinson in Chicago as a transportation systems analyst, and two years running dispatch operations for a regional LTL carrier in São Paulo. He holds a BSc in Industrial Engineering from Universidade de São Paulo (2011) and is fluent in Portuguese, Spanish, and English.

He is the main technical escalation contact for the Bellmark and Cordova accounts and contributes constraint-library feature requests directly to Marcus Oyelaran's roadmap. In 2026 he is building a self-guided pilot tool to shorten deployments from 90 to 45 days.
