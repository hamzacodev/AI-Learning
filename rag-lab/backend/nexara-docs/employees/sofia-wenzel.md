# Sofia Wenzel — Head of Product, TrackPulse

**Name:** Sofia Wenzel
**Role:** Head of Product
**Join date:** 12 October 2020
**Product team:** TrackPulse
**Location:** Berlin, Germany (remote)
**Reports to:** Ingrid Halvorsen, CEO

Sofia Wenzel runs product for TrackPulse and manages a cross-functional team of 27, including engineering, design, and the Austin-based integrations squad. She joined Nexara in October 2020 as a senior product manager, eleven months before TrackPulse entered public beta, and was the product lead on that 8 September 2021 launch.

Prior to Nexara, Sofia spent four years at Forto (formerly FreightHub) in Berlin as a product manager on customer visibility tooling, and two years at Zalando in category operations. She holds an MSc in Logistics and Supply Chain Management from Copenhagen Business School (2014).

Sofia drove the integration of Vessent Analytics' ETA models after the July 2022 acquisition, which reduced road-freight ETA mean absolute error from 51 minutes to 23 minutes over eighteen months. She also owns the carrier-integration roadmap, which grew supported telematics vendors from 19 at GA to 62 today.

Her 2026 focus is a self-serve tier for shippers under 500 shipments per month, planned for limited release in Q4 2026. She sits on Nexara's pricing committee alongside the CFO.
